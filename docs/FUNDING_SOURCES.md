The bilingual directory data is `static/funding.json`. Its `sources` field retains secondary links where needed. Exact programme names and concise summaries are maintained in that data rather than duplicated here. Reviewers should recheck official terms before any release; this file is a research record, not a legal eligibility opinion.

The eight financing-route explanations are general education, with ownership, repayment, time, eligibility and suitability trade-offs. No currently open grant is invented. The regulatory crowdfunding statement cites the [CBUAE regulation](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/entiresection/1577), whose scope excludes financial free zones. Other crowdfunding models/jurisdictions require their applicable regulator and a licensed platform; Khotwa supplies no platform approval.
