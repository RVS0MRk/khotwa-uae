# Khotwa (خطوة) — short demo

**Demo idea:** Mobile car-wash service. Use mocked or fictional presentation data only; do not describe it as real customer evidence.

1. Open Khotwa — From idea to first step.
2. Enter the idea and select a target customer, UAE region, business context, and budget.
3. Generate the plan.
4. Show the Business Snapshot, First Step Today, Opportunity Score, and Before You Spend section.
5. Show Proof Mode and explain that user-entered entries update the preliminary score and decision. Only record genuine customer feedback during real use; empty Proof Mode does not prove no interviews happened elsewhere.
6. Show the 7-day plan, Risk Radar, Customer Personas, Pitch Generator, UAE resources, and PDF report.
7. Switch to Arabic and back. Confirm the business idea, user-entered budget, score and Proof Mode values stay the same without a second Groq generation.
8. Open the Khotwa Advisor and ask a region-specific question. Check that the user budget is labelled USER INPUT, prospects are hypothetical until independently found, and regulatory guidance points to official verification.
9. Export both PDF languages and inspect all pages, especially the Proof Mode summary and mixed Arabic/English business name.
